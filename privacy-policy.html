Privacy Policy

Information about how CatWebs handles website enquiries and related information.

1. Introduction

CatWebs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains the general information we may collect when you contact or use the CatWebs website and how that information may be used.

2. Information You Provide

If you submit an enquiry, you may provide information such as your name, business or organisation name, phone number, email address, location, website and project details.

3. How Information Is Used

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, understand project requirements, communicate about requested services and maintain the website.

4. Forms and Third-Party Services

Website enquiry forms may use third-party form-processing services. Information submitted through those services is handled according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. WhatsApp and External Links

Links to WhatsApp, Google and other external services take you to third-party platforms. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only as reasonably necessary for communication, service delivery, legal obligations and legitimate business records.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated when the website, services or applicable requirements change.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact support@catwebs.in.
